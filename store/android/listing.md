# Kharcha: Google Play store listing

## App name (30 max)
Kharcha: Auto Expense Tracker

## Short description (80 max)
Track spending from your bank SMS. Private, on your phone, no sign-up.

## Full description (4000 max)
Kharcha turns the payment SMS your bank already sends into a clear picture of your money. No typing every expense, no account, no server.

HOW IT WORKS
• Kharcha reads bank, card, UPI and wallet payment alerts on your phone.
• Each payment is sorted into a category: food, shopping, transport, bills and more.
• See what you earned, spent and saved this month, and how it compares with earlier months.

WHAT YOU GET
• Home: money in, money out and savings at a glance
• Activity: every payment in one list, with search and filters for UPI, cards and cash
• Insights: spending by month, by category and by day of the week
• Credit cards: bill amount, minimum due and due date from your card statement SMS
• A monthly budget and savings goals
• Add cash expenses and income by hand
• Light and dark themes
• More than one currency: payments in other currencies are shown, but never mixed into your totals

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything is worked out on your phone. Kharcha has no server and no account.
• Chats from people are dropped before the app reads them. OTPs and offers are ignored.
• No ads, no analytics, no trackers.
• Android cloud backup is turned off, and you can delete all your data with one tap.

SMS ACCESS
Kharcha asks for permission to read SMS so it can find your payments. It only reads messages; it never sends or deletes any. You can say no and add entries by hand instead, or turn SMS access off any time in Android Settings.

Kharcha is a personal tracker. It does not move money, give loans or connect to your bank account.
